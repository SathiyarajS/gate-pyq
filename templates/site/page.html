An honest preview

This archive covers CS papers from 1991–2026. The 1990 paper and separate IT papers from 2004–2008 are out of scope. Engineering mathematics and other excluded subjects remain visible in the thirteenth collection.

Questions without accepted independent transcription review retain paraphrase text and reviewed crops where enabled. Refer to source papers for exact wording, complete option sets, diagrams and shared data. Some historical sources are third-party reproductions with unverified fidelity. Archive links may open an archive rather than an individual PDF; the named member and physical page identify the source.

Keys, derivations and uncertainty

Official answers are displayed exactly as recorded. “None located” means no official key has been found. Independently derived answers are not official matches. Conditional reasoning and unresolved discrepancies remain visible with the solution. Practice checking uses only an unambiguous recorded official key; otherwise use self-check.
