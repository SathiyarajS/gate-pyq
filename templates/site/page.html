{{ title }}

What is here

Every GATE Computer Science question from {{ inventory_years[0] }} to {{ inventory_years[-1] }} ({{ total_questions }} questions in {{ paper_count }} papers), shown as the original question from the paper. Browse by subject, topic or paper, or search from the home page. Each subject also has two PDFs: one with answers and solutions, one with questions only.

Answers

“Official answer” is the published GATE key. Where no key was published, the answer is our own and says so. Where our working differs from the official key, both are shown with a short note.

Similar questions

Questions solved the same way are grouped; “Similar questions” on a question opens the whole group. “Asked again” marks questions GATE repeated, either as they were or with different numbers. “One per method” in the filters keeps only the newest question of each group.

Syllabus

“Not in {{ syllabus.syllabus }} syllabus” marks topics the official {{ syllabus.syllabus }} CS syllabus does not include.

Offline

Save subjects or papers on the Offline page to study without a connection.

Your practice data

Attempts and bookmarks are kept only in this browser. There is no login. Page visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, without cookies or personal data.

Report a mistake

Use “Report issue” on any question, or see the project on GitHub for sources and how the site is built.
