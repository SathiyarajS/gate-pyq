# Explanation and question-access quality

The collection contains 3335 source-linked entries. Independent answer/derivation review is separate from explanatory depth. 1580 explanations have independently reviewed teaching expansions; 1755 remain pending a detailed teaching-quality audit. No entry is certified complete by word count alone.

Current diagnostic: median 74 words per solution, 993 under 50 words. These measurements prioritize inspection; a longer explanation is not automatically correct or sufficient.

## Required depth review

Each solution must identify the given facts and goal, explain the approach, show intermediate reasoning/calculations or a proof, state the result and relevant assumptions, and explain meaningful option distinctions when appropriate. Code, diagrams, SQL and automata requested by a question must be supplied or accurately represented, not replaced with an answer letter. Avoid filler and irrelevant repetition.

## Question access

Entries link original/reproduced PDF pages. Expanded entries include self-contained paraphrases of essential givens, options and diagram relationships, but complete question transcription and accessible figure rendering remain uncertified. Some older official sources are reached through a bulk archive; convenient direct access still requires audit. Third-party reproductions remain fidelity-uncertain. The [2019 Q10 accessible floor-plan reconstruction](question-access/CS-2019-Q10/question.md) is independently reviewed, including all four choices and room topology; it does not infer physical dimensions. The 2025 CS1 Q9 exact fold artwork remains pending.
