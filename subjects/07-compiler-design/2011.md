**Question:** [Official archived paper page 10](https://web.archive.org/web/20220204093545id_/https://gate.iitkgp.ac.in/documents/gatepapers/2011/cs_2011.pdf#page=10); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Official answer:** D

**Derived answer:** D

**Source access:** Archived official IIT Kharagpur paper and IIT Madras key, booklet A. Physical PDF page numbers are used; code A is the matching key column. Final-revision designation is unverified.

## Solution

A leaf needs 1 register; a binary node with equally demanding children needs one more than either, otherwise it needs the larger requirement when evaluated in the favorable order. Thus a−b needs 2; c+d needs 2; e−(c+d) also needs 2; the root combining two2-register subtrees needs 3. Three suffice by retaining one subtree result while evaluating the other in two registers. Two cannot hold one completed child while evaluating the other fixed subtree. Hence D. This evaluates the supplied tree without algebraic reassociation; rewriting the arithmetic expression into a different tree changes the problem.
