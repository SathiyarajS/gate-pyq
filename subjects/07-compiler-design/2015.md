## Solution

The grammar-symbol portion of a shift-reduce stack is a viable prefix: a prefix of a right-sentential form that does not pass the handle’s right end. Taking a shorter prefix cannot cross that end, so every prefix is viable too. Thus C.

**Derived answer:** C

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

At entry to block2, r and u are used by v=r+u; old v is overwritten and old q is overwritten in block4. At entry to block3, s and u are used by q=s*u, while v and r are needed by block4. Thus live-in sets are{r, u} and{s, u, v, r}; their intersection is{r, u}, C. Values overwritten in block1 before any later use need not be added.
