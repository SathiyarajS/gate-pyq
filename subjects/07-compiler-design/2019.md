**Official answer:** 31 to 31

**Derived answer:** 31

## Solution

From A→BD, add FIRST(D) without epsilon, namely d, to FOLLOW(B). Since D is nullable, also add FOLLOW(A)={a}. Thus FOLLOW(B)={a, d}. Their displayed indices are3 and 1, giving31 in descending order.

# GATE 2019 CS: Q53 (printed CS Q43)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 14](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2019/cs_2019.pdf#page=14); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 5 to 5

**Derived answer:** 5

## Solution

GOTO on the opening parenthesis advances S→·(L) to S→(·L). Closure then adds L→·L, S and L→·S. The latter adds S→·(L) and S→·id. Re-expanding L introduces no new items. Thus there are exactly five distinct LR(0) items. The comma in L→L, S is a terminal and remains in that item.
