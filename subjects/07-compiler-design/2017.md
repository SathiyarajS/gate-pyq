**Official answer:** 1024.0 to 1024.0

**Derived answer:** 1024

**Source access:** Archived July2017 original official paper and key. Page numbers refer to the paper PDF; key is a separate two-page PDF.

## Solution

Each sequential if-then-else contributes two structural control-flow alternatives. For ten such statements the number of syntactic control-flow paths is 2^10=1024. This counts paths in the control-flow graph; the grammar alone does not guarantee that every path is feasible for actual values of repeated or constant conditions.

# GATE 2017 CS1: Q62 (printed CS Q52)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 23](https://web.archive.org/web/20170712112248id_/http://www.gate.iitg.ac.in:80/2017questions/CS1.pdf#page=23); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Official answer:** 2.0 to 2.0

**Derived answer:** 2

**Source access:** Archived July2017 original official paper and key. Page numbers refer to the paper PDF; key is a separate two-page PDF.

## Solution

Evaluate b+c with two registers, divide by immediate3, then load d into the freed register and add it. Keep this right-subexpression result in one register; load a into the other and subtract immediate1. Multiply the two registers. No spill occurs, so two registers suffice. At least two are necessary for the operation b+c because both operands are non-immediate and arithmetic cannot use a memory operand.
