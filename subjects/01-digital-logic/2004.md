## Solution

Write the BCD inputs A,B,C,D with weights8,4,2,1. For valid digits0–9, the threshold predicate digit≥5 is A OR[B AND(C OR D)]. **Independently derived answer:** B under conventional parallel complemented-signal implementation

## Solution

With input complements supplied, pi=ai XOR bi and its complement can each be formed in two gate levels, while gi=ai bi needs one. Expanding each carry as an OR of generate/propagate products adds two levels after the propagate signals, so carries and their complements can be ready at time4 (parallel OR/NOR outputs). A sum si=pi XOR ci can then be realized in two further AND–OR levels using these true/complement signals, giving time6. This is the conventional gate-delay model behind B; it assumes sufficient fan-in for the stipulated two-level carry network and parallel generation of needed complements. The wording does not specify a unique netlist or minimum-area implementation; adding serial inversion or a three-level XOR implementation can produce a different delay, so6 is not a delay guaranteed for every allowed circuit.
