**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** B

## Solution

Factor AB+C=(A+C)(B+C). Compute u=NOR(A,C), v=NOR(B,C), then NOR(u,v)=(A+C)(B+C), using three gates. Two do not suffice: with at most two gates, the final NOR receives only primary inputs and the output of one preceding NOR; exhaustive truth-table enumeration of all such input choices finds no realization. Thus3, B. The accompanying check script verifies the lower bound without assuming complementary inputs are supplied.

## Solution

The only transition ending in state01 with output1 is from state10 on input1. From00, reaching10 requires at least two inputs:00 --1/0-->01 --0/0-->10. Appending1 reaches01 and emits1. Thus input101 achieves the target in3 steps, and the predecessor argument rules out shorter inputs. Answer A.
