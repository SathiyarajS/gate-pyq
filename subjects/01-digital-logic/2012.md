**Official answer:** D

**Derived answer:** D

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Two four-bit operands supply eight address bits, so a ROM table needs 2^8=256 entries. Their product ranges up to 15×15=225 and needs eight output bits. Total ROM capacity is 256×8=2048 bits=2 Kbits, D, using binary K.

# GATE 2012 CS: Q40 (printed CS Q30)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 7](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Use Gray-order rows cd=00, 01, 11, 10 and columns ab=00, 01, 11, 10, treating blank cells as zero. The four corners form a wraparound group using the don’t-care at row10, column10, giving b̄d̄. Rows00 and 01 in the two outer columns form a second wraparound group using the don’t-care at row01, column00, giving b̄c̄. Their union covers all required ones without covering a zero. No single implicant can cover both required cells with cd=01 and cd=10 without including row11 zeros. Thus the minimal expression is b̄d̄+b̄c̄, B.
