# GATE 2021 CS1: Q52 (printed CS Q42)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 35](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2021/cs_2021.pdf#page=35); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** B; C; D

**Derived answer:** B; C; D

## Solution

The visually verified expression is F=(X+Y+Z)(NOT X+Y)(NOT Y+Z). If Z=0, the product is (X+Y)(NOT X+Y)(NOT Y)=Y(NOT Y)=0. If Z=1, it is NOT X+Y. Thus F=Z(NOT X+Y) and its complement is NOT Z+X(NOT Y), option B. By (a+b)(c+b)=ac+b, C is the same expression. Direct De Morgan expansion gives X(NOT Y)+Y(NOT Z)+(NOT X)(NOT Y)(NOT Z), exactly D. Option A is false, for example at X=0, Y=1, Z=0: it evaluates to 0 while the complement of F is 1.

**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

## Solution

Use state s to remember the previous input bit, initialized0. The next state is therefore t=b. Output1 is permitted only when both the previous and current bits are1; the first 1 after 0 is suppressed. Thus y=s·b, giving B. The example and option overbars were visually checked.

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 86](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2021/cs_2021.pdf#page=86); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 6 to 6

**Derived answer:** 6

## Solution

The supplied cofactors force on-set minterms0, 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15 and off-set4, 5, 10. Minterms2, 3 are unspecified and may be used as dont-cares for minimization. One minimum SOP is (NOT x)(NOT y)+xy+wz, containing6 literals. No one-literal implicant avoids all forced zeros. Moreover on-set points0, 6, 13 cannot be covered pairwise by a single implicant: their covering cubes would include a forced zero. At least three terms, each with at least two literals, are required, proving the lower bound6. Treating unspecified cofactors as dont-cares is the minimization convention used here. The supplied equations do not uniquely specify f: for example assigning f(0, 0, 1, 0)=1 and f(0, 0, 1, 1)=0 requires a separate three-literal implicant for minterm2, while minterms1, 14, 13 force the three essential prime implicants (NOT x)(NOT y), xy, wz respectively. That completion needs 9 literals, not6. Thus the official6 is the minimum over permissible completions, not a forced value for every function satisfying the three equations.
