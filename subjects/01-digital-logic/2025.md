

# GATE 2025 CS1: Q24

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 24](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=24); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** B;D

**Derived answer:** B;D

## Solution

X is the majority-of-three function. For B, if a=b both sides equal a; if a≠b, X(a, b, z)=z, so both sides equal c. For D, X(a, c, c)=c. If a=c the left majority and the right majority both equal a; if a≠c the left majority equals b and the right side is X(a, b, c)=b. A fails at (a, b, c, d, e)=(0, 0, 0, 1, 1), where its sides are 0 and 1. C fails at (a, b, c, d)=(0, 1, 1, 1), where its sides are 1 and 0. Thus B, D. An exhaustive truth-table check over the five Boolean inputs confirms these identities and counterexamples.

## Solution

Extending a negative two’s-complement value requires copying its sign bit, here 1. Thus −6 becomes 11111010 in eight bits and 1111111111111010 in sixteen bits, options B, D. Numerically these encode 250−256=−6 and 65530−65536=−6. A instead encodes 138−256=−118; C encodes 32778−65536=−32758.

## Solution

Partition first by output pairs: {A, B, C, E} output00; {D, H} output10; {F} output11; {G} output01. A, C have next-state pattern(F,{A, B, C, E}), whereas B, E have({D, H},{A, B, C, E}), so split the first block into{A, C} and{B, E}. Under the refined partition, A, C both transition toF and{B, E}; B, E both to{D, H} and{A, C}; D, H both toG and{A, C}. No further split occurs. The five equivalence classes are AC, BE, DH, F, G, so 5 states are necessary and sufficient.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 7 to 8

**Derived answer:** 8

## Solution

The visually inspected feedback is complemented Q3 into D0, with Q0→D1→D2→D3 as a shift. Thus T(q0, q1, q2, q3)=(¬q3, q0, q1, q2). After four clocks every bit is complemented; after eight the initial vector returns. Every orbit period divides8, but periods1, 2, 4 would imply T⁴ fixes the vector, impossible since it complements it. Hence every initial state belongs to a cycle of 8 distinct states. If counting only new states after leaving the initial state and before returning, there are7. The official accepted range7–8 accommodates that counting distinction.

# GATE 2025 CS2: Q31

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 25](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS22025.pdf#page=25); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A;B;C

**Derived answer:** A;B;C

## Solution

The upper gate is NAND with inputs X, Y; the middle path is NOT X; the lower AND receives X and NOT Y. Their outputs feed OR, so F=NOT(XY)+NOT X+X NOT Y. De Morgan gives NOT(XY)=NOT X+NOT Y, and absorption removes the extra X NOT Y term. Hence F=NOT(XY). A is the minimized form, B its expanded redundant form, and C the direct circuit expression. D fails for X=Y=1, where D gives 1 but F gives 0. Thus A, B, C. All wiring junctions and complement bars were visually inspected.

# GATE 2025 CS2: Q34

**Type / marks:** NAT, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 250 to 250

**Derived answer:** 250

## Solution

Each ripple-counter stage divides its input frequency by 2. Four stages therefore divide by 16, so the last flip-flop’s64 microsecond period is 16 input periods. The input period is 64/16=4 microseconds, giving frequency1/(4×10⁻⁶)=250000 Hz=250 kHz. The question’s counter frequency is read as its input clock frequency, rather than the 15. 625-kHz final-stage output.

# GATE 2025 CS2: Q50

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 38](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS22025.pdf#page=38); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** B;C;D

**Derived answer:** B;C;D

## Solution

B is the consensus identity AB+¬AC+BC=AB+¬AC. Expanding C’s left side gives AB+¬AC+BC, reducing by the same identity. For D, apply De Morgan to the complemented product: the four terms become ¬ABD, ¬C¬D, A¬C¬D, and ¬A¬BD. The first and fourth combine into ¬AD; the third is absorbed by ¬C¬D. This gives the printed right side. A is false: at A=0, B=0, C=1 its left side is 0 while its right side is 1. Select B, C, D. All bars, especially the overall complement in D and its third factor, were visually inspected.
