

# GATE 2006: Q8 (printed CS Q8)

**Question:** [Third-party reproduction](https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/wp-content/cdn-uploads/20211005203119/GATE2006.pdf#page=1), physical page 1. Consult the reproduced wording and choices. Original official provenance and completeness have not been established.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** A under the intended two-phase storage model

## Solution

In A the first D storage stage is enabled by inverted clock, receives ¬f, and exposes its complemented output, restoring f. The second stage is driven by the original clock. Under a level-sensitive two-phase interpretation, a change of f at the falling edge propagates through the first stage during the low half-cycle and becomes visible through the second at the next rising edge: delay T/2, or180 degrees of the clock. D gives the wrong polarity; B and C use the opposite phase order. If these are literal falling-edge-triggered flip-flops, f changes at the sampling edge and setup/hold timing is unspecified, so reliable half-cycle delay is not guaranteed. The reproduction says clocked D flip-flops without resolving this distinction.

**Independently derived answer:** Proven cover given; option letter unresolved

## Solution

For a two-level AND-OR cover to avoid static-1 hazards on single-input changes, every adjacent pair of ON minterms must share a cube. The adjacent pairs are(5,7),(5,13),(7,15),(11,15),(12,13),(13,15). The cover{xz,wyz,wx¬y} covers all six and exactly the ON set. Equivalently, the six edge cubes are{¬wxz,x¬yz,xyz,wyz,wx¬y,wxz}. Option A lacks a cube spanning13↔15; option C leaves12↔13 unbridged. The first term of GFG option D is too blurry to verify as wx¬y, while GateForum prints wzy, duplicating wyz and omitting minterm12; its option B is also not a cover of the stated ON set. Thus a corrected D containing wx¬y would work, but neither reproduction warrants silently claiming that printed D is correct. Original wording/key are needed to resolve the letter.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** B

## Solution

Two’s-complement overflow occurs only when the operands have the same sign and the result has the opposite sign. With sign bits a,b and result sign c, the condition is ab¬c+¬a¬bc, option B. The carry out alone is not signed overflow, and the result sign is not the carry into the sign position.
