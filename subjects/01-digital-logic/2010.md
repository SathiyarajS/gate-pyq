

# GATE 2010: Q16 (printed CS Q6)

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** A

## Solution

The expression is PQ+Q R̄+P R̄, with variable order P,Q,R. PQ covers minterms 6 and 7; Q R̄ covers 2 and 6; P R̄ covers 4 and 6. Taking their union gives m₂+m₄+m₆+m₇, option A. Repeated minterm 6 is included only once.

# GATE 2010: Q18 (printed CS Q8)

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** A

## Solution

The 16-bit pattern F87B represents 0xF87B−0x10000=−1925. Multiplication by 8 gives −15400, which is within the signed 16-bit range. Its two’s-complement encoding is 65536−15400=50136=0xC3D8, A; equivalently shift F87B left three positions and retain 16 bits.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** A

## Solution

The top second-level NOR gives complement(complement(P+Q)+complement(Q+R))=(P+Q)(Q+R)=Q+PR. The bottom gives (P+R)(Q+R)=R+PQ. The final NOR complements their sum: f=complement(Q+PR+R+PQ)=complement(Q+R), since PR is absorbed by R and PQ by Q. Thus A.

# GATE 2010: Q42 (printed CS Q32)

**Question:** [Official-source archive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view), member CS/CS2010.pdf, physical page 7. Consult original wording and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** A

## Solution

Both T inputs are tied to 1, so each flip-flop toggles on a positive clock edge. Q₀ toggles on every external edge; Q₁ is clocked by Q₀ and therefore toggles only on Q₀ transitions 0→1. Starting at Q₁Q₀=00, the settled states after successive external edges are 11,10,01,00. Option A; the ripple clock is not a common synchronous clock.
