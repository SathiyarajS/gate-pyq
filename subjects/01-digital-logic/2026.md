# GATE 2026 CS1: Q22

**Status:** Partial subject-year coverage. Independently derived and matched against the published official key; independent second review and final-key revision check remain pending.

**Question summary (paraphrased):** For 8-bit sign-magnitude X=10110100 and Y=01001100, identify overflowing operations among X+Y, X-Y, -X+Y, and -X-Y.

Operations in the listed order are options A, B, C, D.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 1 mark. Paper variant CS1 (forenoon); the official key labels the exam session 3.

**Official answer:** B;C

**Derived answer:** B;C

## Solution

Seven magnitude bits give the representable interval [-127,127]. X has sign 1 and magnitude binary 0110100 = 52, hence X=-52; Y=76. The four mathematical results are 24, -128, 128, and -24, respectively. Only the middle two fall outside the interval. Therefore B and C overflow. Sign-magnitude is essential: treating these words as two’s-complement integers would solve a different problem.
