## Solution

Step 2: In four bits, 0111+0001=1000 overflows the signed range even though there is no final carry. Conversely, 1111+0001=0000 with a carry represents−1+1=0 without signed overflow. Conclusion and qualifications: If a≥0 and b<0 are representable, their sum lies between b and a and remains representable. Thus opposite-sign addition cannot overflow, B. For two’s complement, overflow is the XOR of the carry into and out of the sign position, not simply the outgoing carry or equality of those carries.

Label the six external wires a,b,c,d,e,f from top to bottom. These are local wire labels, not extra assumptions about their values.
