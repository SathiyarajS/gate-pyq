**Independently derived answer:** A

## Solution

Group the four corner cells using the bottom-right dont-care to obtain b′d′. Group the top/bottom cells in the left two columns to obtain a′d′. Together these cover every required1 and no specified0, giving b′d′+a′d′, A. A single implicant cannot include both the ab=01 and ab=10 required regions without covering an ab=11 zero, so two terms are needed.

## Solution

The middle factor is P(Q′+R), so the full expression is0 when P=0. Set P=1: the first factor is1, the middle Q′+R, and the last P′R′+Q′ reduces to Q′. Their product is Q′. Thus the whole function is PQ′, A.
