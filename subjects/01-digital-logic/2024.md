**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

## Solution

Five-bit two’s complement represents −16 through15. A=10 and B=26−32=−6. A+B=4, A−B=16, B−A=−16, and 2B=−12. Only16 is outside the representable interval, so B is correct; −16 is representable, not underflow.

# GATE 2024 CS1: Q28

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 18](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2024/CS124S5.pdf#page=18); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A;C

**Derived answer:** A;C

## Solution

The visually inspected circuit ANDs X with NOT X. Without gate delays, Y=X·NOT X=0. When X rises, the direct input becomes1 before the inverter output falls from 1 to 0, so both AND inputs can briefly be1 and generate a positive pulse if delays permit it. On a falling transition the direct input becomes0 immediately, and Y remains0; this is not a transient negative excursion from a high steady output. Thus A, C. The word transient denotes a glitch, not simply an interval at the normal zero level.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** C;D

**Derived answer:** C;D

## Solution

The circuit first ANDs f1 and f2, whose common minterms are{3, 5, 7, 11, 13}. The OR of f3, f4 has minterms{0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 11, 13}. The final XOR keeps their symmetric difference, cancelling11, 13 and giving{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7}. Its complementary maxterm indices are8 through15. Hence C, D. The final gate was visually confirmed to be XOR, not OR.
