**Derived answer:** B

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Let R mean rain and M mean match played. The premise is R→¬M. Its contrapositive M→¬R proves inference I1. From ¬R alone one cannot infer M: the match could be cancelled for another reason. I2 denies the antecedent and is invalid. Hence B.

**Derived answer:** B

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The given graph consists of one five-vertex cycle, with a pendant edge and a two-edge pendant path attached to the same cycle vertex. That attachment vertex has degree four; two vertices have degree one and the other five have degree two. Option B has exactly this structure: its curved outer edge completes the five-cycle, and its central degree-four vertex supports the one-edge upward branch and the two-edge lower-right branch. Map the attachment vertex, then the two distinct-length branches, then the five-cycle in order to obtain an isomorphism. A has a triangle and D a four-cycle; C attaches its pendant branches at different cycle vertices. Hence B.

# GATE 2012 CS: Q43 (printed CS Q33)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 7](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

If the first roll is 6, success occurs immediately with probability 1/6; first rolls 4 or 5 stop unsuccessfully. For first roll 1, the second must be 5 or 6 (two outcomes); for 2 it must be 4, 5, 6 (three); for 3 it must be 3, 4, 5, 6 (four). Each ordered two-roll result has probability 1/36. Total success probability is 1/6+(2+3+4)/36=15/36=5/12, B. One-roll and two-roll terminal outcomes are not equiprobable.

## Solution

Each of n domain elements has two choices, giving 2^n functions. A function is not onto precisely when all n elements map to one fixed codomain element. There are two such constant functions. Subtract them to obtain 2^n−2 surjections, C.

**Official answer:** Marks to All

**Derived answer:** 45 (no listed option)

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Choose the four vertices in C(6, 4)=15 ways. On a chosen set, cyclic rotations and reversal represent the same undirected cycle, so there are 4!/(4×2)=3 distinct cycles. The total is 15×3=45. None of the listed 15, 30, 90, 360 equals 45. Counting opposite orientations separately would give 90, but that is not the usual convention for distinct undirected cycles.
