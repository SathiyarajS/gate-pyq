## Solution

But n=1 or 2 gives a unique tree, which is why the unprinted size restriction must stay explicit. Neither a different tree shape nor a different traversal order changes total cost here.

## Solution

R∩S is reflexive and symmetric because both relations are; if x(R∩S)y and y(R∩S)z, both transitivity rules give x(R∩S)z. Union need not be transitive: partitions {1, 2}|{3} and {1}|{2, 3} yield 1 related to 2 and 2 to 3 in the union but not 1 to 3. Thus only intersection is guaranteed, C.

Union can chain one edge from R and another from S without containing the resulting third edge. The three-element partition counterexample above shows why B/A are not guaranteed.
