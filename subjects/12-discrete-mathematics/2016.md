**Official answer:** 2.0 : 2.0

**Derived answer:** 2

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2016.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

For even n halve n. For odd n>5, applying the odd rule and then halving sends n to(n+5)/2<n. Thus every value eventually reaches1..5. The numbers1, 2, 3, 4 are connected by the cycle1→6→3→8→4→2→1, while5→10→5 forms another component. Divisibility by 5 is invariant under both rule equalities, so these components never merge. A function constant1 on nonmultiples of 5 and constant2 on multiples satisfies both rules and realizes two values. Therefore the maximum range size is 2.
