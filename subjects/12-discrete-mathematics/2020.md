## Solution

## Solution

The two indistinguishable Ls must occupy two of positions2, 4, 5, giving three choices. The one unoccupied position among those three is the original location of one of I, A, C. Place I, A, C in that position and positions1, 3: of the 6 permutations, exactly2 put that particular character back in its original position. Thus each L placement permits4 arrangements, totaling3*4=12. Direct enumeration of the 60 distinct permutations also yields12.

**Official answer:** 0.5 to 0.5

**Derived answer:** 0.5

## Solution

Because a is nonzero, choose an index j with a_j=1. Pair each vector x with the vector obtained by flipping only x_j. This is a fixed-point-free bijection on all2^n equally likely inputs, and it reverses the parity of the dot product. Exactly half of the inputs yield an odd sum, so the probability is 1/2. The argument in fact works for any n≥1 with nonzero a.

# GATE 2020 CS: Q62 (printed CS Q52)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 15](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2020/cs_2020.pdf#page=15); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Official answer:** 7 to 7

**Derived answer:** 7

## Solution

The new vertex s has degree7, so at least7 edge colors are needed. A concrete7-coloring proves sufficiency. Call the parts a 0, a 1, a 2 and b0, b1, b2, b3. Give edges s-a 0, s-a 1, s-a 2 colors1, 2, 3, and s-b0,..., s-b3 colors4, 5, 6, 7. For the three rows of edges ai-bj, use colors (2, 3, 4, 5), (1, 4, 3, 6), and (5, 1, 2, 4), respectively. Each row and column has distinct colors and avoids its endpoint edge to s. Thus this is a proper edge-coloring with 7 colors, proving the minimum7.
