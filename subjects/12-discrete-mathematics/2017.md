**Question:** [Official archived paper page 5](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A

**Derived answer:** A structurally; B logically equivalent

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2-2017.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The direct translation is(¬p∧r)∧(¬r→(p∧q)), option A: only if makes ¬r the antecedent. However option B is(¬p∧r)∧((p∧q)→¬r). Under the first conjunct, both implications are true, so both A and B simplify to¬p∧r. Thus A matches the syntactic translation, but the options are not semantically unique. The official key accepts only A.

Nonemptiness lets us choose an x, whose promised y establishes I. Quantifier negation makes IV exactly ∀x∃yR(x, y), the premise. II need not hold: equality on a two-element domain has no common y for all x. III need not hold: R(x, y) iff y=0 on domain {0, 1}. Therefore I and IV, B.

# GATE 2017 CS1: Q39 (printed CS Q29)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 14](https://web.archive.org/web/20170712112248id_/http://www.gate.iitg.ac.in:80/2017questions/CS1.pdf#page=14); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** D

**Derived answer:** D with false-valuation interpretation

**Source access:** Archived July2017 original official paper and key. Page numbers refer to the paper PDF; key is a separate two-page PDF.

## Solution

If (p→q)→r is false at the considered valuation, r=false and p→q=true. Then (r→p)→q reduces to q, so it is true when q is true, D. With p=false, q=false, r=false the premise is false and the target is false, ruling out C; q=true permits a true target, ruling out B. Literal terminology is imprecise: with independent atomic p, q, r the first formula is not a contradiction (false under every valuation), since r=true makes it true. The intended premise is that its value is false.
