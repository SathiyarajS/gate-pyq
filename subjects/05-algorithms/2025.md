**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

## Solution

Every path in the spanning tree is also a path in the original graph, because T is a subgraph of G. Minimizing over all G paths cannot yield a larger distance than the sole T path, hence d1(u, v)≤d2(u, v), B. Equality can occur, e. g. G already a tree. Strict inequality can occur in a unit-weight triangle whose tree omits the direct edge between u and v. The premise that a spanning tree exists supplies connectedness.

# GATE 2025 CS1: Q33

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 33](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=33); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 435 to 435

**Derived answer:** 435

## Solution

This is bubble sort. Every swap exchanges an adjacent inverted pair and decreases the inversion count by exactly one. A descending sequence of 30 distinct numbers has every pair inverted, so its inversion count is C(30, 2)=30×29/2=435. The final ascending sequence has zero inversions, hence exactly 435 swaps occur. Equivalently the passes perform 29+28+⋯+1 swaps.

# GATE 2025 CS1: Q43

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 43](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=43); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** C

**Derived answer:** C

## Solution

A BFS tree rooted at r has height h=max_v d(r, v). For diameter endpoints u, v, the triangle inequality gives 30=d(u, v)≤d(u, r)+d(r, v)≤2h. Thus h≥15, option C. An arbitrary root need not realize either endpoint or a graph centre, so exact height15 or30 is not guaranteed. The finite all-pairs diameter is read in the usual connected-graph sense.

# GATE 2025 CS1: Q64

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 64](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=64); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 5 to 5

**Derived answer:** 5

## Solution

For the cut{B, D}|{A, C}, crossing edge weights are x (BC), 6 (DA), 7 (BA), 8 (DC). If x<6, BC is the unique lightest cut edge and must belong to every MST. At x=6, the alternative spanning tree BD(1), AC(3), AD(6) has total 10 without BC, tying the tree using BC instead ofAD. For x>6 the same alternative is cheaper than replacingAD byBC; equivalently BC is strictly heaviest on cycle B-D-A-C-B and cannot be required. The maximum integer x is therefore5.
