## Solution

The weight1 edges form an acyclic forest and all are included before any weight2 edge by Kruskal. In the left component, contraction leaves three parallel weight2 choices joining its two weight1 components. The long horizontal bridge of weight2 is mandatory. In the right triangle, the weight1 edge is mandatory and either of the two weight2 edges connects the third vertex. Thus independent choices give3·2=6 MSTs. The remaining left weight2 edges internal to contracted components cannot be used.

# GATE 2014 CS3: Q23 (printed CS Q13)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 56](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 19 to 19

**Derived answer:** 19

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2014.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

A recursive DFS stack is a simple path. A path spanning both3×3 grids passes through the central connector and cannot include its pendant leaf. It could use9 left-grid vertices, the connector, and at most9 vertices from the right grid plus its pendant together. The latter bound follows from checkerboard parity: entering the right grid at its bottom-left black vertex, a Hamilton path through all9 would end black, whereas its pendant attaches to the white middle-right vertex. Thus including that pendant prevents using all9 grid vertices. Hence depth≤19. Snake paths through all9 vertices of each grid joined by the connector attain19, by choosing the corresponding DFS neighbor order.
