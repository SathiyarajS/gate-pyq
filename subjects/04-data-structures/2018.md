**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2018.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Inserting at the head updates a constant number of pointers, Θ(1). Removing the tail requires locating its predecessor, which a singly linked list with only head/tail pointers does not directly supply; traversing n nodes costsΘ(n) in the worst case. Thus B.

# GATE 2018 CS: Q56 (printed CS Q46)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 19](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 80 to 80

**Derived answer:** 80

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2018.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The minimum1 must be the root. The complete seven-node shape has three positions in each subtree. Choose which three of the other six keys go left in C(6, 3)=20 ways. In either three-node subtree its smallest key is the root and its two leaves can be ordered in 2 ways. Thus there are20*2*2=80 heaps.
