# GATE 2015 CS1: Q60 (printed CS Q60)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 22](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A

**Derived answer:** A

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Let k=sqrt(log N). With an unsorted array, N insertions take O(N) amortized and k arbitrary-key finds take O(Nk); pointer-directed deletion and decrease-key need at most O(Nk) total even if compaction work is charged. Total O(Nsqrt(log N)). A standard online binary min-heap costs O(Nlog N) for N worst-case insertions, and arbitrary-key finds are still linear. Maintaining sorted arrays or lists can cost Θ(N²) over the N insertions. Thus the unsorted array is best among the listed textbook online structures, A. This comparison is for the specified operation model, not an alternative offline bulk-sorting/heap-building algorithm.

**Derived answer:** A for worst-case heap repair

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Sift the root down, swapping with the larger child until order is restored. The path can have Θ(log n) levels, and a smallest root with heap-ordered descendants realizes that worst case. Thus the intended worst-case lower bound is Ω(log n), A. It is not a per-instance lower bound: a root already exceeding its children needs only constant checking.

Three swaps along the ancestor chain move100 through indices 13→6→3→1, producing a valid max-heap. Fewer cannot suffice: 100 must reach the root. If swapped directly with 89, the resulting89 at 13 exceeds both ancestors15 at 6 and 50 at 3; one additional swap cannot repair both (swapping with 3 leaves50 below15; swapping with 6 leaves89 below50). If100 reaches the root in two swaps, at least one of the remaining displaced chain values is still out of heap order. Thus the minimum is 3, D.
