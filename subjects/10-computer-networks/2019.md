# GATE 2019 CS: Q59 (printed CS Q49)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 15](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2019/cs_2019.pdf#page=15); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 3 to 3

**Derived answer:** 3

## Solution

To connect k switches into one LAN needs at least k−1 inter-switch links, consuming2(k−1) ports. Thus at most8k−2(k−1)=6k+2 ports remain for machines. Two switches offer only 14 machine ports, insufficient for15. Three switches in a chain offer20, and machines can be distributed over them without exceeding8 ports at any switch. Therefore the minimum is 3.

# GATE 2019 CS: Q64 (printed CS Q54)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 16](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2019/cs_2019.pdf#page=16); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 97 to 97

**Derived answer:** 97

## Solution

For the two-prime RSA modulus n=pq, phi(n)=(p−1)(q−1)=n−p−q+1. Thus p+q=3007+1−2880=128 and pq=3007. The quadratic t²−128t+3007=0 has discriminant128²−4*3007=4356=66², giving primes(128±66)/2=97 and 31. The factor greater than50 is 97.
