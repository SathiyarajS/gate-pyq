**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** D

## Solution

Let payload size be p bytes, so each packet has size p+3 and there are 24/p packets. Across three equal-rate store-and-forward links the serialization makespan, apart from constant propagation terms, is (24/p+2)(p+3)=30+2p+72/p byte-times. Its derivative is 2−72/p², giving p=6 bytes. This divides 24 exactly, and the total packet size is 6+3=9 bytes, option D. Packet size includes the header; six is only the payload.
