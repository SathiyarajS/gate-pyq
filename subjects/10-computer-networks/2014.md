**Official answer:** D

**Derived answer:** D under the standard protocol comparison

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2014.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Link-state routing maintains topology and runs a shortest-path computation, conventionally costing more local computation but converging faster than iterative distance-vector updates. Split horizon does not eliminate all loops involving three or more routers, so S2 is false. The textbook comparison is S1/S3 true, D; actual convergence depends on timers, implementation and failure pattern, not the node/link counts alone.
