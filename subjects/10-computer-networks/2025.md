**Question:** [Official archived paper page 57](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=57); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 7 to 7

**Derived answer:** 7

## Solution

Using the ordinary20-byte IPv4 header, the largest8-byte-aligned nonfinal payload at MTU5000 is floor((5000−20)/8)×8=4976. A15000-byte payload becomes4976, 4976, 4976, 72. At the second router MTU3000 allows2976 aligned payload bytes, so each4976-byte fragment splits into 2976+2000, while72 stays intact. Routers do not coalesce the existing fragments. Thus2+2+2+1=7 fragments reach the destination. If15000 includes the original header the last payload is 52 instead of 72, leaving the same count. The header-length convention does not alter the requested count for ordinary legal IPv4 header lengths. Both MTUs and topology were visually inspected.
