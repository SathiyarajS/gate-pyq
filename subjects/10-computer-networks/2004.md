**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** B

## Solution

After A succeeds, its next frame starts with collision count reset; following the next collision A chooses uniformly from{0,1}. B’s still-unsent frame has now collided twice, so B chooses uniformly from{0,1,2,3}. Of the8 equally likely pairs, A wins strictly when(A,B) is(0,1),(0,2),(0,3),(1,2),(1,3), giving5/8=0.625. Equal choices cause another collision, not a win. This evaluates the stated next backoff race, not eventual success after arbitrarily many tied races.
