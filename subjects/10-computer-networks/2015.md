**Question:** [Official archived paper page 10](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B under nonshrinking-window textbook model

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Sequence numbers count bytes, so I is false. RTO includes estimated RTT plus a nonnegative variability margin, making II true in the usual model. The receiver-advertised window varies, so III is false. With a nonshrinking receive-window right edge, outstanding data respects the advertised window, giving IV true and false statements I and III, official B. The word always in IV needs qualification: a receiver can shrink its window after data was sent, leaving more outstanding bytes than its new advertisement. TCP discourages shrinking but implementations must tolerate it. Therefore B assumes the simplified flow-control model.
