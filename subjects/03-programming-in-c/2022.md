## Solution

Initially x=1 and z={10, 11}. Assigning 10 through p=&x changes x to 10. After p points to z[1], the expression *(&z[0]+1) denotes that same second element; adding 3 changes it to 14. z[0] stays 10. The printed values are 10, 10, 14, D.
