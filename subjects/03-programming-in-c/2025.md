# GATE 2025 CS2: Q19

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 17](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS22025.pdf#page=17); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** D

**Derived answer:** D

## Solution

The loop copies the 12 non-null characters of Hello World! from indices 2–13 into indices 0–11, but stops before copying the terminator at index14. The original characters at indices 12 and 13, d and !, remain; the original null at 14 also remains. Printing a therefore gives Hello World!d!, followed by the requested newline, option D. This user-defined forward loop safely reads ahead of its writes; it is not a call to the standard strcpy function with overlapping arguments.
