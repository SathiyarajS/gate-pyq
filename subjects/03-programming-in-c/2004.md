**Independently derived answer:** A

## Solution

After i iterations, n is the original prefix d1...d_(m−i), and rev is the processed suffix in reverse order d_m d_(m−1)...d_(m−i+1), A. Each step removes n’s final digit and appends it to rev. The notation describes numeric digit values, so leading zeros in the reversed suffix do not change the value; assume the input is supplied nonnegative and the reversed value fits int.

**Question:** [Third-party reproduction](https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/wp-content/cdn-uploads/20211005203330/GATE2004.pdf#page=6), physical page 6. Consult the reproduced wording and choices. Original official provenance and completeness have not been established.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** Not available; no official key located.

**Independently derived answer:** C

## Solution

For positive x,y, each iteration replaces the larger of m,n by its difference with the smaller. This preserves gcd(m,n), keeps both positive, and strictly decreases m+n until m=n. The printed common value is gcd(x,y). This is not ordinary repeated subtraction for division: the roles of the two operands change.
