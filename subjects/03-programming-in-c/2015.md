# GATE 2015 CS1: Q62 (printed CS Q62)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 23](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MCQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A

**Derived answer:** A under intended address display; literal printf has undefined behavior

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Each row occupies3*4=12 bytes. x+3 points to the fourth row at 2000+36=2036; *(x+3) decays to that row’s first element at 2036. *(x+2)+3 is the valid one-past pointer of the third row, also numerically2036 under the stated layout. Thus the intended address triple is 2036, 2036, 2036, A. However all three arguments are pointers while %u requires unsigned int; this variadic format mismatch is undefined behavior in C. A conforming address display uses %p with each pointer cast to void*, whose textual format is implementation-defined. The omitted stdio declaration is another standalone-code caveat.

**Official answer:** B

**Derived answer:** B

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Initially q=0, r=x, so x=qy+r. Each iteration replaces r by r−y and q by q+1, preserving that invariant. Positive y decreases nonnegative r on every iteration; termination gives 0≤r<y. Hence the listed postcondition x=qy+r and r<y is B; the full invariant also supplies the nonnegative lower bound.

**Derived answer:** 15

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The function-local static stkTop starts at 0 and shadows the global stkTop, so assigning the global variable−1 does not affect the stack. After configuring size 10 and pushing5 then10, the two pops return 10 and 5 in whichever operand-evaluation order is chosen. Their sum is 15. Although the + operands have unspecified order, the two function executions are indeterminately sequenced rather than interleaving their bodies; this is not the direct unsequenced-increment case.
