**Official answer:** 10 to 10

**Derived answer:** 10

## Solution

b remains a+4 throughout, so *b=10. At iteration i, *(b−i)=a[4−i]=10−2i. The summand is therefore(10−i)−(10−2i)=i. Summing i=0, 1, 2, 3, 4 gives 10. The code subtracts i; it does not decrement b, and all accessed pointers remain within the array.
