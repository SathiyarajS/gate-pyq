**Paraphrased problem context:** Choose the pair that does NOT consist of synonyms. A: aversion, dislike. B: luminous, radiant. C: plunder, loot. D: yielding, resistant.

## Solution

1. Compare the meanings within each pair, rather than looking for a word that differs from the other six. Aversion and dislike both express an unfavorable feeling toward something.

**Paraphrased problem context:** How many roots of e^x+0.5x²-2=0 lie in [-5,5]? A:0. B:1. C:2. D:3.

## Solution

1. Set f(x)=e^x+x²/2-2. It is continuous, with f(-5)=e^-5+10.5>0, f(0)=-1, and f(5)=e^5+10.5>0. The intermediate value theorem therefore gives at least one root in (-5,0) and one in (0,5).

2. To rule out more roots, differentiate twice: f′(x)=e^x+x and f″(x)=e^x+1>0 everywhere. Hence f′ is strictly increasing and f is strictly convex.

3. Three distinct roots would, by Rolle's theorem on consecutive root intervals, force f′ to vanish twice, contradicting its strict increase. There are exactly two roots in the requested range, C.

2. Then ((x+y)−(x−y))/2=2y/2=y, which is the smaller value. If x<y, |x−y|=y−x, giving ((x+y)−(y−x))/2=x.

3. Both cases return min(x,y), including equality where either value is the same. Thus B. A quick check x=2,y=5 gives (7−3)/2=2.

**Paraphrased problem context:** A contour map has 25 m contour intervals. In the figure, P lies inside the 575 m contour of the left hill; Q lies between the 525 and 550 m contours of the right hill; R lies between 475 and 500 m; S is in the small low region between 450 and 475 m; T lies between 500 and 525 m. At flood-water level 525 m, which villages submerge? A P,Q; B P,Q,T; C R,S,T; D Q,R,S. These height bands are a textual description of the source figure.

## Solution

1. A contour line represents a fixed elevation. Starting from the labelled 500 and 550 m curves and using 25 m spacing identifies intermediate 525 m curves; points inside a hill’s higher contours are higher, not lower.

2. P is above 575 m and Q is above 525 m, so neither lies below the flood level. R is below 500 m, S lies in the low region below 475 m, and T lies between 500 and 525 m.

3. All of R,S,T have elevation less than 525 m and are submerged under the exam’s level-water assumption. Hence C. A closed contour alone does not establish a hill; the height labels and surrounding contours determine the low region at S.
