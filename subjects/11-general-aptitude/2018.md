## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

The conjunction “but” introduces a contrast, not a synonym. An organized investigation follows a plan; a meandering one wanders from topic to topic. Timely describes when something happens, while consistent and systematic support rather than oppose organized work. Thus the contrast in the whole sentence, rather than the positive phrase “yield new facts” alone, determines the choice.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

Meandering means wandering or lacking a direct plan, contrasting with the organized investigations described as more successful. Thus A.

**Paraphrased problem context:** A square has area d. A circle uses its diagonal as diameter. Find the circle area: A πd; B πd²; C πd²/4; D πd/2.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

Let the side be s, so s²=d. Pythagoras applied to two adjacent sides gives diameter²=s²+s²=2d. A circle area is π times radius squared, not diameter squared: radius²=(diameter/2)²=2d/4. As a dimensional check, the result must be proportional to d; terms proportional to d² have the wrong area scaling.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

A square of area d has side√d and diagonal√(2d). The circle radius is half that diagonal, so its area isπ(2d)/4=πd/2, D.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Find the smallest natural number leaving remainder 7 when divided by any of 20, 42, 76. Choices: A 3047; B 6047; C 7987; D 63847.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

Subtracting the common remainder converts three congruences to divisibility conditions. Factor 20=2²·5, 42=2·3·7, and 76=2²·19. Take the highest exponent of each prime, not the product of all three divisors. All nonnegative solutions are 7+7980t for integers t≥0. The intended option uses t=1; checking the boundary t=0 exposes the wording defect rather than a calculation error.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

The congruences say N−7 is divisible by 20, 42, 76. Their least common multiple is 2²·3·5·7·19=7980. The least solution with positive quotients is 7987, C. However7 itself is a natural number and leaves remainder7 on division by all three divisors with quotient0. The source omits a condition such as N greater than the divisors, so the literal smallest answer is 7, absent from the options.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

Check ratios of successive nonzero terms: 12/2=6, 60/12=5, 240/60=4, 720/240=3, and 1440/720=2. Continuing these integer multipliers with 1 then 0 accounts for both the blank and the supplied final term. Multiplying immediately by 0 would omit the next multiplier. As with finite sequence puzzles, this is the intended simple pattern, not a uniqueness theorem over arbitrary sequence rules.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

Successive multipliers are6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. The missing term is 1440·1=1440, followed by 0, B. This uses the intended descending-multiplier sequence rule.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Among 300 eligible seniors, each man may claim Rs 750 and each woman Rs 1000. Fractions claiming are 8/9 of men and 2/3 of women. Find total rupees paid: A 150000; B 200000; C 175000; D 151000.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

Do not assume 150 men and 150 women. Let their eligible counts be M and F. Multiplying each award by its claiming fraction gives the payout per eligible person of that category: 750·8/9=2000/3 and 1000·2/3=2000/3. These coefficients are equal, so the unknown split cancels. The fractions describe the recipients, not reductions in the individual award.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

Let M+F=300. The payout is 750·(8M/9)+1000·(2F/3)=(2000/3)(M+F)=200000 rupees, B. Therefore the male/female split need not be known.

**Official answer:** C

**Derived answer:** C under nonunit positive-base assumption

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2018.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Given pqr≠0, p^(−x)=1/q, q^(−y)=1/r, r^(−z)=1/p, find xyz. Choices: A −1; B 1/(pqr); C 1; D pqr.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

Under the usual positive nonunit-base interpretation, taking reciprocals gives p^x=q, q^y=r and r^z=p. Logs therefore give x ln p=ln q, y ln q=ln r, z ln r=ln p. Multiplication cancels only when these logarithms are nonzero. That condition is not implied by pqr≠0. For example, choosing all bases 1 and x=y=z=2 satisfies the written equations but yields product 8.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

For positive p, q, r different from 1, the equations give x=log q/log p, y=log r/log q, z=log p/log r. Multiplying cancels the logarithms and gives xyz=1, C. But the stated condition pqr≠0 allows p=q=r=1; then all three equations hold for arbitrary x, y, z, so the product is undetermined. The official answer needs a nonunit-base/domain qualification.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

Use triangle angle sums together with supplementary angles on straight lines. Label α=∠BAD, β=∠DEC, γ=∠BFC. Because AD and AE are the same ray and EC and EB are the same ray, triangle AEB has angles α and β at A and E. At B, extending AB to BF changes its interior angle to its supplement. At C, extending DC to CF supplies the second supplementary-angle relation. No lengths or apparent angle sizes are needed.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

Let α=angle BAD, β=angle DEC, γ=angle BFC. From triangle AEB, angle ABE=180°−α−β. Since BA and BF are opposite rays, angle CBF=α+β. Triangle BCF gives angle BCF=180°−α−β−γ. Since CD and CF are opposite rays, angle BCD=α+β+γ. Hence β+γ=angle BCD−angle BAD, A. Collinearities were checked in the original diagram.

**Paraphrased problem context:** A fair six-faced die has four green and two red faces. After seven independent rolls, which count is most likely? A 3 green/4 red; B 4/3; C 5/2; D 6/1.

## Solution

Method and intermediate reasoning:

The event specifies a count, not a particular ordering. Therefore include the binomial factor: P(K=k)=C(7,k)(2/3)^k(1/3)^(7−k). For the four options, the numerators over the common denominator 3^7 are 280, 560, 672 and 448. Five greens has the largest numerator. Simply maximizing the probability of an individual ordered sequence would incorrectly ignore how many arrangements produce each count.

Derivation, answer and qualifications:

If K counts green faces, K is binomial with n=7, p=2/3. Its unique mode is floor((7+1)·2/3)=5. Equivalently consecutive probability ratios are P(K=k+1)/P(K=k)=2(7−k)/(k+1), above1 through k=4 and below1 at k=5. Five green and two red is most likely, C.
