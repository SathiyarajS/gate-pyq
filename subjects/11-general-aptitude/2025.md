## Solution

Undo each diagonal fold by reflection of every cut across that fold. The triangular notch on the outer edge gives four triangular edge cuts whose apices point outward toward the nearest square edge. The cut meeting one diagonal unfolds into an axis-aligned square at each of two opposite near-corner positions; the other cut gives the reflected right-angle shapes at the other two positions. This is A. B rotates the squares into diamonds, C reverses triangular orientations, and D incorrectly puts a square in the centre. Fold lines, cut outlines and all options were visually inspected.
