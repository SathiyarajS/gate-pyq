## Solution

The key treats erection cost as proportional to wage rate and total working period: 13200×(6/5)×(23/24)=15180, B. If working hours means hours per day while the required total labour remains fixed, the number of days instead grows24/23 and the labour cost becomes13200×(6/5)×(24/23)≈16528.70, not an option. The wording does not distinguish those interpretations; retain B as the intended model, not an unconditional consequence.
