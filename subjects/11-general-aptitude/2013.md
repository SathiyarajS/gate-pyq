**Paraphrased problem context:** Choose the closest meaning of “nadir”: A highest; B lowest; C medium; D integration.

## Solution

Nadir means the lowest point, the opposite of zenith. Hence lowest, B.

Teaching detail:
Use the direction of the word rather than its emotional connotation: a nadir is a bottommost point, whether literal or figurative (for example, the nadir of someone’s fortunes). “Highest” reverses that relation, “medium” is intermediate, and “integration” describes combination rather than position. Thus only lowest preserves the meaning.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Find the maximum sum of an initial segment of 44, 42, 40,… . Options A502, B504, C506, D500.

## Solution

The successive terms decrease by 2. Include all positive terms44, 42,..., 2; adding0 changes nothing and subsequent negative terms reduce the sum. There are22 positive terms, with sum22(44+2)/2=506, C.

Teaching detail:
Let S_m be the sum of the first m terms. Its next increment is 44−2m. While this is positive, taking another term increases the total; once it is negative, taking it decreases the total. The positive terms stop at 2, so there are (44−2)/2+1=22. The arithmetic-series formula gives S_22=22×46/2=506. Taking the following zero term ties this maximum, but no later prefix exceeds it.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Complete “Were you a bird, you ___ in the sky.” A would fly; B shall fly; C should fly; D shall have flown.

## Solution

Were you a bird is an inverted unreal conditional: If you were a bird, you would fly. Thus A.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Choose the grammatically incorrect sentence: A He is of Asian origin. B They belonged to Africa. C She is an European. D They migrated from India to Australia.

## Solution

European begins with the consonant sound /y/, so the phrase is a European, not an European. The incorrect sentence is C.

Teaching detail:
The indefinite article depends on the initial sound, not merely the initial letter. “European” is pronounced with an initial /j/ sound, like “you,” so it takes “a.” “Asian” begins with a vowel sound but option A uses the uncountable phrase “of Asian origin,” which needs no indefinite article. The prepositions in the other listed sentences are grammatical. The required incorrect sentence is therefore C.

For total distance d, the three portions are d/2, d/4, d/4. Time=d/120+d/120+d/40=d/24 hours, so the overall speed is 24 km/h, C. An arithmetic average of the three speeds would incorrectly weight the travel times.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Erecting a structure currently costs Rs13, 200. Labour wages per day rise by one fifth, while working hours decrease by one twenty-fourth of the current period. Find the new cost: A16, 500; B15, 180; C11, 000; D10, 120 rupees.

## Solution

The key treats erection cost as proportional to wage rate and total working period: 13200×(6/5)×(23/24)=15180, B. This assumes the stated reduction concerns total labour time and that the entire relevant cost scales as labour. If working hours means hours per day while the required total labour remains fixed, the number of days instead grows24/23 and the labour cost becomes 13200×(6/5)×(24/23)≈16528.70, not an option. The wording does not distinguish those interpretations; retain B as the intended model, not an unconditional consequence.

Teaching detail:
Under the key’s labour-cost interpretation, the wage multiplier is 1+1/5=6/5 and the total labour-period multiplier is 1−1/24=23/24. Apply both to the old cost: 13200×6/5=15840, then15840×23/24=15180. This arithmetic is internally consistent only with the stated intended model. If the shorter hours are per day and the task requires unchanged total work, fewer hours per day require more paid days, producing the inverse time multiplier instead.
