**Paraphrased problem context:** The cost function for a product in a firm is given by 5q², where q is the amount of production. The
       firm can sell the product at a market price of 50 per unit. The number of units to be produced by
       the firm such that the profit is maximized is
        (A) 5                   (B) 10                  (C) 15                  (D) 25

## Solution

Revenue is 50q and cost is 5q², so profit is 50q−5q²=125−5(q−5)². Its maximum occurs at q=5, which is also a feasible integer quantity. Thus A.

Worked check / additional reasoning: At q=0 profit is zero, whereas q=5 gives revenue 250 and cost 125, hence profit 125. Maximizing revenue alone would ignore the quadratic cost; π′(q)=50−10q and π″(q)=−10 independently verify the unique maximum.

       sentence:

        Despite several ––––––––– the mission succeeded in its attempt to resolve the conflict.
        (A) attempts            (B) setbacks            (C) meetings            (D) delegations

## Solution

Despite introduces a circumstance that would normally hinder the outcome. Setbacks hinder a mission, yet it succeeded. Attempts, meetings and delegations do not supply that direct contrast. Hence setbacks, B.

Worked check / additional reasoning: The clause after the blank states a positive outcome, success. The introductory word despite signals that the blank should describe an opposing circumstance, not simply an activity that happened during the mission.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Which one of the following options is the closest in meaning to the word given below?

        Mitigate
        (A) Diminish            (B) Divulge             (C) Dedicate            (D) Denote

## Solution

To mitigate is to make something less severe or intense. Diminish has the closest meaning. Divulge means disclose, dedicate means devote, and denote means signify. Hence A.

Worked check / additional reasoning: For example, mitigating damage means reducing damage rather than revealing or describing it. This usage distinguishes diminish from divulge even though both can be verbs applied to an unpleasant situation.

**Paraphrased problem context:** Choose the grammatically INCORRECT sentence:
        (A) They gave us the money back less the service charges of Three Hundred rupees.
        (B) This country’s expenditure is not less than that of Bangladesh.
        (C) The committee initially asked for a funding of Fifty Lakh rupees, but later settled for a lesser
            sum.
        (D) This country’s expenditure on educational reforms is very less.

## Solution

Less is comparative and is not normally modified by very in standard written English. The intended correction is very low or much less, depending on whether a comparison is intended. The subtraction use of less in A, not less than in B, and lesser sum in C are valid uses. Thus D is the intended incorrect construction.

Worked check / additional reasoning: The adjective low describes an absolute expenditure level and accepts very. The comparative less instead requires a comparative modifier such as much. Thus very low and much less are grammatical repairs with different meanings.

        Suresh’s dog is the one ––––––––– was hurt in the stampede.

## Solution

The missing relative pronoun is the subject of was hurt, so whom is unsuitable. Who may be used for an individually regarded pet in some contexts. The item therefore need not have a uniquely grammatical choice. The official scoring entry is Marks to All; this language analysis is independent and is not an official explanation of that decision.

Worked check / additional reasoning: To test case, replace the relative clause by “the dog was hurt”: the missing pronoun stands where a subject would stand, not where an object would stand. That eliminates whom but does not uniquely separate that and which.

**Paraphrased problem context:** A political party orders an arch for the entrance to the ground in which the annual convention is
       being held. The profile of the arch follows the equation y = 2x – 0.1x² where y is the height of the
       arch in meters. The maximum possible height of the arch is
        (A) 8 meters            (B) 10 meters           (C) 12 meters           (D) 14 meters

## Solution

Complete the square: y=2x−0.1x²=10−0.1(x−10)². Since the squared term is nonnegative, the greatest height is 10 metres, attained at x=10 metres. Hence B.

Worked check / additional reasoning: The parabola opens downward because its x² coefficient is negative. Its zeros are x=0 and x=20, so the vertex lies midway at x=10; substituting gives 20−10=10 metres, within the arch’s nonnegative-height span.

**Paraphrased problem context:** An automobile plant contracted to buy shock absorbers from two suppliers X and Y. X supplies
       60% and Y supplies 40% of the shock absorbers. All shock absorbers are subjected to a quality test.
       The ones that pass the quality test are considered reliable. Of X’s shock absorbers, 96% are reliable.
       Of Y’s shock absorbers, 72% are reliable.

        The probability that a randomly chosen shock absorber, which is found to be reliable, is made by Y
        is
        (A) 0.288               (B) 0.334                (C) 0.667                (D) 0.720

## Solution

Let R mean reliable. P(Y∩R)=0.40×0.72=0.288 and P(R)=0.60×0.96+0.40×0.72=0.864. Bayes’ rule gives P(Y|R)=0.288/0.864=1/3. Option B lists 0.334, the closest offered value, although ordinary three-decimal rounding of 1/3 is 0.333. The exact mathematical answer is 1/3; retain official B.

Worked check / additional reasoning: For a concrete frequency check, imagine 1000 components: 600 come from X and 400 from Y. Reliable counts are 576 and 288, so the conditioning pool contains 864 components, not all 1000. The numerator alone, 0.288, is the joint probability and corresponds to distractor A.

**Derived answer:** C

**Paraphrased problem context:** Which of the following assertions are CORRECT?

        P: Adding 7 to each entry in a list adds 7 to the mean of the list
        Q: Adding 7 to each entry in a list adds 7 to the standard deviation of the list
        R: Doubling each entry in a list doubles the mean of the list
        S: Doubling each entry in a list leaves the standard deviation of the list unchanged
        (A) P, Q                (B) Q, R                 (C) P, R                 (D) R, S

## Solution

For transformed observations z_i=a x_i+b, the mean becomes a times the old mean plus b and standard deviation becomes |a| times the old standard deviation. Adding 7 changes the mean by 7 but leaves deviations from the mean unchanged. Doubling doubles both mean and standard deviation. P and R are true, Q and S are not general identities. Hence C.

Worked check / additional reasoning: Writing μ′=aμ+b gives z_i−μ′=a(x_i−μ). Squaring and averaging multiplies variance by a²; taking the square root gives |a|σ. The same transformation rule holds for sample standard deviation because its denominator is unchanged.
