**Official answer:** C

**Derived answer:** C

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2012.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

For transformed observations z_i=a x_i+b, the mean becomes a times the old mean plus b and standard deviation becomes |a| times the old standard deviation. Adding 7 changes the mean by 7 but leaves deviations from the mean unchanged. Doubling doubles both mean and standard deviation. P and R are true, Q and S are not general identities. Hence C.
