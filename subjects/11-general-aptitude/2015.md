**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

Exactly three passes has probability0.5−0.4=0.1, and exactly one has probability0.75−0.5=0.25. The expected number of passes is m+p+c=0.25+2(0.4)+3(0.1)=1.35=27/20, so I is true and II false. The mathematically correct choice is A (I only), whereas the visible official answer is D (I and III).
