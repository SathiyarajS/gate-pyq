Then 731_8+672_8 has units 3, tens 3+7=10 decimal giving digit 2 and carry 1, and hundreds 7+6+1=14 decimal giving digit 6 and carry 1. The result is 1623_8, option C. The available digits are two 2s, three 3s and four 4s. A number above 3000 starts with 3 or 4. After a leading 3, the remaining three positions have 3³ possibilities except 222 and 333, giving 25. After a leading 4, only 222 is forbidden, giving 26; 444 is allowed because four 4s are available overall. Total 25+26=51, B. Equal copies are not distinguished.
