For multiplier1010010010101010, the unequal pairs occur at bit positions1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15 (LSB position0). Thus there are13 arithmetic additions/subtractions in 16 iterations. Do not add a seventeenth sign-extension operation; the signed16-bit algorithm already handles the leading sign bit.
