**Question:** [Official archived paper page 18](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Official answer:** 4.72 to 4.72

**Derived answer:** 4.72

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2-2017.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The weighted L1 miss probability is 0.6*(1−0.8)+0.4*(1−0.9)=0.16. Each L1 lookup costs2 ns; a miss adds8 ns at L2, and an L2 miss adds90 ns in main memory. With the L2 hit rate interpreted conditionally on reaching L2, average time=2+0.16*(8+0.1*90)=4.72 ns. Clean blocks require no dirty eviction write-back, and referred-word-first avoids waiting for the whole block before completing this read.

# GATE 2017 CS1: Q61 (printed CS Q51)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 23](https://web.archive.org/web/20170712112248id_/http://www.gate.iitg.ac.in:80/2017questions/CS1.pdf#page=23); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 76.0 to 76.0

**Derived answer:** 76

**Source access:** Archived July2017 original official paper and key. Page numbers refer to the paper PDF; key is a separate two-page PDF.

## Solution

There are256/2=128 sets. Blocks0, 128, 256 share set0 and 1, 129, 257 share set1. In each eight-reference half of the first repetition, the first three distinct blocks cause compulsory misses and two subsequent references cause conflict misses. Thus the first full repetition has 6 compulsory and 4 conflict misses. Each later repetition has 4 conflict misses per set, or8 total. Across10 repetitions, conflict misses=4+9*8=76. Only six distinct blocks exist, so a fully associative cache of this capacity would have no post-compulsory misses.
