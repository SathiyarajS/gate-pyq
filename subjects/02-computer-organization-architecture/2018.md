**Official answer:** 59.0 to 60.0

**Derived answer:** 59 percent

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2018.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The row refresh time per period is 2^14·50 ns=819200 ns=0.8192ms. The remaining fraction of the 2ms period is 1−0.8192/2=0.5904, or59.04%, rounded59%. The eight parallel4-bit chips refresh a row together to form the 32-bit memory; their refresh times are not added serially.

A block contains2^(M+W) bytes. There are2^(N−M−W)/K sets, so index bits=N−M−W−log2K, and block-offset bits=M+W. Subtract both from P address bits: tag=P−N+log2K, B, under the conventional power-of-two cache parameters.

# GATE 2018 CS: Q60 (printed CS Q50)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 21](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 219 to 219

**Derived answer:** 219

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2018.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

The single PO stage is occupied for40*3+35*2+25*1=215 clocks. The first instruction needs 3 clocks to reach PO through IF, ID, OF, and the final instruction needs 1 WB clock afterward. With no data/control hazards, earlier stages can supply the next instruction whenever PO becomes free, so total=3+215+1=219. This uses the intended non-overlapped PO-stage model; a separately internally pipelined execution unit would need an initiation-interval specification.

# GATE 2018 CS: Q61 (printed CS Q51)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 21](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 32 to 32

**Derived answer:** 32

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2018.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

There are2^16 instruction encodings. A three-integer-register instruction uses2^12 encodings, as does a two-floating-register instruction. Each mixed register instruction uses2^10 encodings. The remaining space is 65536−4*4096−8*4096−14*1024=2048. Each type4 opcode needs 64 encodings, so N≤2048/64=32. This bound is achievable with expanding opcodes: allocate12 four-bit prefixes to types1/2; the remaining 4 prefixes yield16 six-bit prefixes, 14 for type3, leaving2 prefixes expandable to 32 ten-bit type4 opcodes.
