This ignores fill/drain overhead for a finite short instruction sequence, whose length is not supplied.

# GATE 2015 CS2: Q53 (printed CS Q53)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 47](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1popAHO-cX69oQEx_tD0wZXL1tTrBljeO/view); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 13

**Derived answer:** 13

**Source access:** Open archive member CS/CS2015.pdf inside the linked official CS.zip. Page numbers refer to the extracted PDF, not the Drive preview.

## Solution

With a single occupied PO stage and the stated forwarding, MUL uses IF1, OF2, PO3–5, WB6. DIV uses PO6–10, ADD uses PO11 and SUB uses PO12; SUB writes back in cycle13. The dependent ADD/SUB operand fetches use the forwarded results at the end of the preceding producer PO cycle. Thus total 13 cycles. This is the conventional in-order, non-internally-pipelined PO-stage model; independent or internally pipelined functional units would require more scheduling details.
