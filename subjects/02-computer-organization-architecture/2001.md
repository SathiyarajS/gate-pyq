**Essential problem context (paraphrased):** Why does a cache block contain multiple words? Choices: temporal locality, spatial locality, reduced miss penalty, or none of these.

## Solution

Step 1: A reference often predicts references to nearby addresses, for example successive array elements or adjacent instructions. Loading neighboring words with the requested word prepares for those accesses: spatial locality.

Therefore spatial locality is the specific reason for grouping words, B.
