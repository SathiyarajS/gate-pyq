## Required depth review

Each solution must identify the given facts and goal, explain the approach, show intermediate reasoning/calculations or a proof, state the result and relevant assumptions, and explain meaningful option distinctions when appropriate. Code, diagrams, SQL and automata requested by a question must be supplied or accurately represented, not replaced with an answer letter. Avoid filler and irrelevant repetition.
