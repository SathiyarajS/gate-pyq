**Type / marks:** MCQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** D

**Derived answer:** D

## Solution

The curves cross when x³=x, at −1, 0, 1. On the four intervals separated by those points, max(x, x³) equals x, x³, x, x³ respectively. At −1 and 1 the two branch derivatives are1 and 3; at 0 they are0 and 1. Hence all three joins are nondifferentiable and all other points lie on a polynomial branch. Answer D.

Rank-nullity gives dim ker(A)=m−rank(A)≥m−n, proving A. B is too strong: if rank(A)<n the nullspace needs more than m−n basis vectors; a zero matrix is a counterexample. # GATE 2024 CS2: Q47

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 32](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2024/CS224S6.pdf#page=32); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A;B

**Derived answer:** A;B

## Solution

A single row swap negates the determinant, proving A. If det(A) is nonzero then det(B)=−det(A) is nonzero as well, proving B. Symmetry is not preserved: swapping rows of diag(1, 2) gives[[0, 2],[1, 0]], which is not symmetric. Zero trace is not preserved: A=[[1, 2],[3,−1]] has trace0, but its row-swapped matrix has trace5. Answer A, B.
