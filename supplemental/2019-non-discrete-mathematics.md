**Official answer:** 12 to 12

**Derived answer:** 12

## Solution

The product of eigenvalues equals the determinant, counting algebraic multiplicity. R is the Vandermonde matrix with rows[1, t, t², t³] for t=2, 3, 4, 5. Its determinant is the product of pairwise positive differences: (3−2)(4−2)(5−2)(4−3)(5−3)(5−4)=1*2*3*1*2*1=12. Its absolute value is 12.

# GATE 2019 CS: Q57 (printed CS Q47)

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 14](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2019/cs_2019.pdf#page=14); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 2 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 0.8 to 0.8

**Derived answer:** 0.8

## Solution

The quadratic discriminant is (6Y)²−4*3*(3Y+6)=36(Y²−Y−2)=36(Y−2)(Y+1). Both roots are real exactly when this is nonnegative. Within1<Y<6 that means Y≥2, an interval of length 4 out of total length 5. The probability is 4/5=0.8; the boundary point has probability zero.
