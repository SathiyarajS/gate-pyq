

# GATE 2025 CS1: Q23

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 23](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=23); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** MSQ, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** A;D

**Derived answer:** A;D

## Solution

The coefficient determinant is 1−k². For k≠±1 it is nonzero, so there is exactly one solution. At k=1 the two equations demand x+y=1 and x+y=−1, impossible. At k=−1 the equations are x−y=1 and its negative, so infinitely many solutions exist. Exactly one parameter gives no solution and exactly one gives infinitely many; hence A, D.

# GATE 2025 CS1: Q32

**Question:** [Official archived paper page 32](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/download/2025/CS12025.pdf#page=32); consult original wording, diagrams and choices.

**Type / marks:** NAT, 1 mark(s).

**Official answer:** 0.49 to 0.51

**Derived answer:** 0.50

## Solution

Let F mean the fake coin and H mean two heads. With a uniform coin choice, P(F)=1/5, P(H|F)=1, and P(H|not F)=1/4 for the independent tosses of a regular fair coin. Bayes gives P(F|H)=(1/5)/[(1/5)+(4/5)(1/4)]=(1/5)/(2/5)=1/2=0. 50. The official range is 0. 49 to 0. 51.
