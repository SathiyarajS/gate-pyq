## Solution

Since u≠v and Fu=Fv, the nonzero vector u−v lies in the null space. Hence F is singular and det F=0. Every u+t(v−u), t real, is another solution, yielding infinitely many. Singularity does not force identical rows: F=[[1,2],[2,4]] is a counterexample. Therefore D is false as a necessary assertion.
