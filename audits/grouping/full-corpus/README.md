## What the counts mean

- `assessed` includes the blocked question because its source and possible alternatives were investigated.
- `complete` requires readable source review, a recorded grouping decision and completed independent group checking. It therefore remains 3,405 rather than falsely counting the blocked item.
- `no_reviewed_match` is not proof of uniqueness. Finite method comparison cannot guarantee all semantic relationships were found.
- Ambiguous questions are visible and never automatically folded. Grouping does not certify transcription, solution, official key or historical reproduction fidelity.
- Independent checks produced exclusions and wording corrections before integration; reports preserve the original proposals and required changes rather than pretending the first proposals were error-free.
